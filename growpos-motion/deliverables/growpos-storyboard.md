## Editorial notes

The seven sections use slow, consistent camera pushes and direct cuts. Copy sits in the upper safe area and uses the app's green palette with a high contrast white treatment. The video has no voice-over. The sound bed is an original synthesized score with restrained click and confirmation accents.

This is an editable visual proof built from still concept plates. It does not include espresso extraction, cup service, moving people, a live checkout action, or an animated reporting state. See the production report before treating the file as a release master.
