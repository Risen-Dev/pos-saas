## Concept and audio assets

- Six 941 × 1672 café/device plates in `preproduction/assets/generated-backgrounds/` are generated still concepts supplied in the workspace. They are not live-action footage, approved photography, or clean footage plates.
- `growpos-original-score.wav` is synthesized locally by `make-score.mjs` for this film. It is not a commercial recording.
- Subtle clicks and the confirmation accent are synthesized in the score script. No licensed café ambience or third-party sound library was present or added.
- `ui-*.png` in `source/public/reel/` are perspective composites of the verified UI capture images onto the concept device screens.
