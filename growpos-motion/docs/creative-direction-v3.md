# GrowPOS — Everything Flows (V3 direction)

## Product-specific idea

The film follows one real cashier interaction: a product is selected, the order panel updates, and the recorded total appears. A thin GrowPOS green signal resolves into an edge of that same interface. The move then travels out from the interface into a physical laptop scene. The screen remains the source of truth throughout; the stock and report moments use only the corresponding parts of the included demo recording.

The idea is **one continuous operational flow**, rather than a feature checklist. Use “Jualan jadi lebih praktis.” for the cashier beat and “Satu Sistem, Tumbuh Bersama.” for the closing identity. Keep the fictional demo account visible only where it is part of the authentic UI; label any extracted figures as demo data. No phone, completed payment, customer outcome, or public URL is implied by the available assets.

## Motion and composition rules

- Continuous screen-to-device camera move is the acceptance gate; a 2D screenshot zoom does not qualify.
- Green signal direction becomes the camera's direction of travel. Avoid repeating a decorative line over every shot.
- Use one key light, a soft green rim, dark neutral studio, and a restrained floor reflection. Maintain crisp UI, legible typography, and Reels safe areas.
- The laptop is the sole device. Do not add a phone until a real mobile capture exists.
- Use no invented charts, success states, mobile flows, payment confirmations, or merchant results.

## Acceptance gate

Render and inspect the first eight seconds before authoring the remaining shots. Continue only when the real interaction is readable, the screen-to-device move is seamless, device geometry and screen registration hold through camera motion, and the lighting/composition look production-ready at phone size. A rendered file by itself does not pass this gate.
